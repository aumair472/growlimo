## Compliance: HIPAA, AHPRA, and Global Standards

HIPAA-compliant healthcare chatbot requires BAA-signed vendors, source-attributed responses, and seamless human handover for urgent cases. UK/Australia AHPRA & ASA compliance demands clear disclaimers: "Not medical advice; consult professional".

### Non-Negotiable Checklist:
- End-to-end encryption for PHI.
- Minimum necessary data access.
- Audit trails for interactions.
- Explicit opt-outs.
