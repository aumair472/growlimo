## Mistake #1: Targeting the Wrong Keywords

This is the **biggest reason PPC campaigns fail**.

### What Goes Wrong

- Targeting broad keywords  
- Ignoring buyer intent  
- Not using negative keywords  

### Example

Targeting:  
👉 “marketing”  

Instead of:  
👉 “PPC services California”  

---

### Fix

- Focus on high-intent keywords  
- Use long-tail keywords  
- Add negative keywords  

Learn more:  
[How PPC generates instant leads](/blog/how-ppc-advertising-drives-instant-leads)

## Mistake #2: Sending Traffic to the Homepage

This kills conversions instantly.

### Why It Fails

Your homepage is too general and not designed to convert.

---

### Fix

Create dedicated landing pages with:

- Clear offer  
- Strong CTA  
- Trust signals  

Example guide:  
[Healthcare Landing Page Optimization](https://www.growlimo.com/blog/healthcare-landing-page-optimization-maximizing-patient-conversions-roi/)

## Mistake #3: Weak Ad Copy

Even if your targeting is correct, poor messaging will destroy performance.

### Common Issues

- No clear value  
- No urgency  
- Generic messaging  

---

### Fix

Use:

- Strong headlines  
- Benefits-focused copy  
- Clear CTA  

---

## Mistake #4: No Conversion Tracking

If you’re not tracking conversions, you’re flying blind.

### What Happens

- You don’t know what’s working  
- You keep wasting money  

---

### Fix

Track:

- Calls  
- Form submissions  
- Purchases  

---

## Mistake #5: Ignoring Data & Optimization

PPC is not “set and forget”.

### Common Problem

Businesses launch campaigns and never optimize them.

---

### Fix

- Analyze performance weekly  
- Pause underperforming ads  
- Scale winning campaigns  

---

## Mistake #6: Poor Budget Allocation

Spending without strategy leads to poor ROI.

---

### Fix

- Start small  
- Test campaigns  
- Scale only what works
