## What Are PPC Services?

PPC services involve managing paid advertising campaigns across platforms like Google Ads, Meta Ads, and display networks.

You only pay when someone clicks your ad — but success depends on **how well your campaigns are structured and optimized**.

### Core Components of PPC

- Keyword research (high-intent targeting)
- Ad copywriting (conversion-focused messaging)
- Budget allocation
- Landing page optimization
- Conversion tracking
- Continuous optimization
