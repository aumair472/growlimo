## How Google Ads Works

Google Ads operates on a pay-per-click auction model: you pay when someone clicks your ad, and your ad's position is determined by a combination of bid amount and Quality Score (a 1-10 rating Google assigns based on expected CTR, ad relevance, and landing page experience).

### Key Components

- Keywords — the search terms that trigger your ad
- Ad copy — the headlines and descriptions searchers see
- Landing pages — where the click lands, and the single biggest lever on conversion rate
- Bidding strategy — manual CPC, Target CPA, Target ROAS, or Maximize Conversions, each suited to different account maturity levels

Each of these elements must be optimized to achieve maximum ROI — a strong bid strategy paired with a weak landing page still produces a poor CPA, and vice versa.

## Keyword Strategy for Google Ads in California

Choosing the right keywords is critical, and California's search volume means even narrow niches have meaningful traffic to capture.

### Types of Keywords

**High-Intent Keywords**  
- "Google Ads agency California"  
- "PPC services near me"  
- "[service] + [city]" patterns — e.g. "emergency plumber Los Angeles," "personal injury lawyer San Diego" — these convert at 2-4x the rate of generic category terms because the searcher has already narrowed intent by location and urgency

**Long-Tail Keywords**  
- Lower competition, often 40-60% cheaper CPC than head terms in the same category
- Higher conversion rates because the search phrase matches a specific need ("same day AC repair Sacramento" vs. "AC repair")

**Negative Keywords**  
- Prevent wasted budget on irrelevant clicks — "free," "jobs," "DIY," and "how to" are common negative-keyword additions for service businesses that don't want browsers or job seekers clicking a lead-gen ad
- California accounts with no negative keyword list typically waste 15-25% of spend on non-converting clicks within the first month

## Budget Optimization Strategies

Managing your budget effectively is essential, especially in a market where CPCs can run 2-3x the national average.

### Tips

- Start with targeted campaigns — a single tightly-scoped campaign around your highest-value service line will outperform a broad, unfocused account every time in a high-CPC market
- Focus on high-converting keywords — pull search term reports weekly for the first 60 days and reallocate budget toward terms with the lowest CPA, not just the highest volume
- Adjust bids based on performance — dayparting (limiting ads to your actual business hours or peak call times) can cut wasted spend by 10-20% for service businesses that can't answer after-hours leads
- Eliminate underperforming ads — any ad group with a CTR below 2% or a Quality Score below 5 after 200+ impressions needs new ad copy, not just a bid increase

### A Worked Example

A mid-sized home services business in the Bay Area running $8,000/month with no negative keyword list, single broad-match keywords, and one generic landing page will typically see CPAs in the $180-$250 range. The same budget restructured into 3-4 tightly-themed ad groups, long-tail keyword targeting, a dedicated landing page per service, and an active negative keyword list commonly brings CPA down into the $90-$130 range within 60-90 days — without increasing spend. The lever isn't more budget; it's structure.

## Common Google Ads Mistakes in California Accounts

- Targeting broad match keywords without a negative keyword list — the single most common cause of wasted spend in high-CPC California markets
- Poor ad copy — generic headlines that don't reference the searcher's city or specific need underperform localized, benefit-led copy by a wide margin
- Weak landing pages — sending paid traffic to a homepage instead of a service-specific page with a clear CTA is the fastest way to inflate CPA
- No conversion tracking setup — running a California account without call tracking or form-submission tracking means optimizing blind
- Ignoring negative keywords — see budget section above; this alone can account for a quarter of wasted spend
- Not deciding between managing the account in-house vs. hiring a specialist agency before scaling budget — see our comparison of [hiring a Google Ads expert vs. an agency](/blog/google-ads-expert-vs-agency-california/) for how to think through that tradeoff

Avoiding these mistakes can significantly improve ROI — most California accounts we audit are losing 20-30% of spend to one or more of the issues above.

## Advanced Google Ads Strategies

### Audience Targeting

Layer in-market and affinity audiences on top of keyword targeting to bid more aggressively on users already showing purchase intent signals — particularly effective for higher-consideration purchases like legal services, home renovation, and healthcare.

### Remarketing

Reconnect with users who did not convert on their first visit. California's high CPCs make first-click conversion rare for considered purchases — remarketing campaigns typically cost 40-60% less per click than cold search traffic and convert at a meaningfully higher rate because the audience already knows your brand.

### A/B Testing

Test different ads and landing pages continuously. Run at minimum two ad variants per ad group at all times, and let each test reach statistical significance (roughly 100+ clicks per variant) before declaring a winner — pulling the plug early on a "losing" ad is one of the most common ways accounts stall out below their potential ROI.
