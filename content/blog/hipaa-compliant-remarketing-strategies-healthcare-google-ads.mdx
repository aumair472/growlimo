## What is Healthcare Remarketing?

Remarketing is a digital marketing strategy that targets users who have previously interacted with your website. Using the Google Display Network and YouTube, you can show ads to these past visitors as they browse other websites across the internet.

> ⚠️ **The Healthcare Catch:** Google's Personalized Advertising Policy strictly prohibits targeting users based on "personal hardships," which includes physical and mental health conditions. If a patient visits your page on "Clinical Depression" or "Heart Surgery," you cannot follow them around the internet with ads for those specific treatments.

## Key Remarketing Strategies for Medical Practices

### 1. Focus on "Practice-Level" Brand Awareness

Since you cannot use Dynamic Remarketing to show a patient the exact medical procedure they searched for, your remarketing ads must focus on your overall brand authority.

- ❌ **Do Not Say:** *"Still suffering from back pain? Come back and book your spinal adjustment."* — This implies knowledge of their health condition and will trigger an account suspension.
- ✅ **Do Say:** *"Voted Dallas' #1 Orthopedic Clinic. Accepting New Patients. Schedule Your Consultation Today."*

### 2. Segment Your Audience Safely

You can still segment your website visitors based on their general behavior, provided the audience list is large enough to ensure user anonymity (Google requires a minimum of 100 active visitors).

- Target visitors who viewed your "About Our Doctors" page.
- Target users who visited your "Accepted Insurances" page.
- Target users who started to fill out a general contact form but abandoned it.

### 3. Use Compelling, Trust-Building Ad Visuals

- Clear calls-to-action (CTA) like "Meet Our Team" or "Schedule a Consultation."

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**1. Is it legal to retarget patients in healthcare marketing?**

Yes, but with strict limitations. HIPAA laws and Google's Personalized Advertising policies prohibit targeting users based on specific health conditions. You cannot use ads that imply you know a user's medical history. Retargeting must remain broad, focusing on the clinic's brand, location, and general services.

**2. Can I retarget users who visited a specific treatment page?**

Generally, no. If the treatment page implies a specific health condition (e.g., Cancer Treatment, Addiction Rehab, STD Testing), Google will disapprove the ads and potentially suspend your account. Stick to retargeting users who visited general pages like the Homepage, Contact Page, or About Us page.

**3. What is the difference between Google Search Ads and Remarketing Ads?**

Google Search Ads appear when a user actively types a query into Google (e.g., "dentist near me"). Remarketing ads are visual banners or videos that appear on other websites (like news outlets or YouTube) after a user has already visited your website, keeping your clinic top-of-mind.

**4. How do I track remarketing conversions compliantly?**

You must use HIPAA-compliant tracking solutions. Standard Google Analytics pixels can inadvertently capture PHI (Protected Health Information). Clinics should use secure form trackers and HIPAA-compliant call tracking software (like CallRail) that sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA).

**5. How much should a medical clinic spend on remarketing?**

Because Display Network clicks are much cheaper than Search clicks, remarketing should only make up about **10% to 15%** of your total Google Ads budget. It acts as an affordable support system to close leads generated by your primary Search campaigns.
