## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
 
### 1. Is blogging actually effective for doctors and medical clinics?
 
Yes. While the homepage of your website targets broad keywords like "Dentist in Chicago," your blog targets long-tail, specific queries like "How much do dental implants cost in Chicago?" These highly specific searches often convert at a much higher rate because the patient is closer to making a medical decision.
 
### 2. How often should a medical practice publish new blogs?
 
For optimal SEO growth, medical practices should aim to publish 2 to 4 high-quality, comprehensive blog posts per month. Consistency is far more important than publishing a large batch of articles all at once and then ignoring the blog for months.
 
### 3. Who should write the content for a healthcare blog?
 
To satisfy Google's strict E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) guidelines for medical content (also known as "Your Money or Your Life" topics), content should ideally be written by professional healthcare copywriters and strictly reviewed or co-authored by a licensed physician at your practice.
 
### 4. Can we provide medical advice in a blog post?
 
No. You must be very careful to provide medical education, not specific medical advice. Every medical blog post should contain a clear disclaimer stating that the content is for informational purposes only and does not replace a professional consultation with a doctor.
 
### 5. How long does it take for a medical blog post to rank on Google?
 
SEO is a long-term strategy. It typically takes a new blog post 3 to 6 months to mature, index properly, and climb the search engine rankings. However, once a well-optimized post reaches page one, it can generate free, passive patient leads for years.
