## Defining Success Metrics for Healthcare Campaigns

### Industry Benchmarks (USA — Medical & Dental Services)

- **Average CPC (Cost Per Click):** $3.50–$15.00 (varies heavily by specialty)
- **Average Conversion Rate:** 5%–12.9% (Optimized)
- **Average CPA (Cost Per Acquisition):** $40–$150
- **ROAS Potential:** 4x–17x

High-ticket keywords (like "dental implants" or "sports surgeon") have a higher CPC but convert exceptionally well, driving massive revenue per patient.

### Set Clear ROAS Targets

- Target a minimum **4x ROAS** ($4 in patient revenue / $1 in ad spend) for general care.
- High-ticket procedures (e.g., cosmetic dentistry) can easily hit an **8x–17x ROAS** on $5,000+ treatments versus $150 routine checkups.

### Core KPIs for Medical Practices

- **ROAS (Return on Ad Spend):** Primary focus; driven by high-margin service lines.
- **CPA (Cost Per Patient Acquisition):** Aim for $40–$80 for urgent care; $100–$200 for specialized surgeries.
- **CVR (Conversion Rate):** Optimized medical landing pages should push conversion rates past 10%.

## Local Targeting: Slash Wasted Ad Spend

Healthcare is hyper-local. Patients generally look for "ready to book" doctors nearby. By focusing on strict geographic radii, clinics can save **30–50% of their ad spend** that would otherwise be wasted on out-of-town clicks.

## Ad Scheduling: Align with Peak Patient Demand

Schedule your ads to run during your clinic's business hours so your front desk can answer live calls. Cut off-peak hours to prevent wasting ad spend on patients who go to voicemail and never call back.
