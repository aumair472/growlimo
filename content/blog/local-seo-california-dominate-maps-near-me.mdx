## What Is Local SEO?

Local SEO is the process of optimizing your business to appear in location-based searches.

It focuses on:

- Google Maps rankings  
- “Near me” searches  
- Local listings  
- Location-specific keywords  

This strategy is essential for businesses that rely on local customers.

---

## How Google Maps Ranking Works

Google ranks local businesses based on three key factors.

### Relevance  
How well your business matches the search query.

### Distance  
How close your business is to the user.

### Prominence  
How well-known and trusted your business is online.

Understanding these factors is critical if you want to rank in the top 3 map results.
