## Cost Comparison: Agency vs In-House

### In-House Costs (Monthly)

- Salary: $4,000 – $8,000  
- Tools: $300 – $1,000  
- Training: ongoing  

👉 Total: $5,000 – $10,000+

---

### Agency Costs (Monthly)

- Management fee: $1,000 – $3,000  
- No hiring cost  
- No training cost  

👉 More cost-efficient for most businesses
