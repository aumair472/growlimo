## Key Factors to Consider When Choosing a PPC Agency

### 1. Proven Results (Case Studies)

Always ask:

👉 “Can you show real results?”

A reliable agency will provide:

- Case studies  
- Performance metrics  
- ROI improvements  

Example:  
[Physiotherapy Google Ads San Francisco Case Study](https://growlimo.com/case-studies/physiotherapy-google-ads-san-francisco/)

---

### 2. Industry Experience

Different industries require different strategies.

For example:

- Healthcare PPC is highly competitive  
- eCommerce requires different funnel optimization  

---

### 3. Transparency & Reporting

Avoid agencies that:

- Hide data  
- Provide vague reports  

A good agency gives:

- Clear dashboards  
- Regular performance updates  

---

### 4. Strategy, Not Just Execution

Many agencies just “run ads”.

The right agency:

- Builds strategy  
- Optimizes continuously  
- Focuses on ROI  

---

### 5. Communication & Support

You need:

- Regular updates  
- Clear communication  
- Fast response time

## Red Flags to Avoid

### 🚫 No Case Studies

If they can’t prove results, don’t trust them.

---

### 🚫 Unrealistic Promises

“No.1 ranking overnight” or “guaranteed leads”

---

### 🚫 Lack of Transparency

If you don’t see data, you’re at risk.

---

### 🚫 One-Size-Fits-All Strategy

Every business needs a customized approach.
