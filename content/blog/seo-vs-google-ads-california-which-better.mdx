## When SEO Is the Better Choice

SEO is ideal if you want long-term growth.

### Choose SEO When

- You want consistent traffic  
- You have time to build authority  
- You want to reduce ad spend  
- You aim for long-term ROI

## When Google Ads Is the Better Choice

Google Ads is ideal for immediate results.

### Choose Google Ads When

- You need leads quickly  
- You are launching a new business  
- You want to test keywords  
- You have a flexible budget
