## Implementing FAQ Schema Markup Healthcare

JSON-LD FAQ schema healthcare unlocks rich snippets, boosting CTR by 20-30%. Pairing schema with a strong [Healthcare PPC Services](/healthcare-ppc-services-texas/) strategy accelerates both paid and organic visibility simultaneously.​

### Essential Template:
Paste this script into the &lt;head> section of your specific page. Replace the placeholder text with your actual questions and answers.
````
&lt;script type="application/ld+json">
{
 "@context": "https://schema.org",  
 "@type": "FAQPage",  
 "mainEntity": [{    
  "@type": "Question",    
  "name": "Does [Clinic] accept new patients in [City]?",    
  "acceptedAnswer": {      
   "@type": "Answer",      
   "text": "Yes, book online or call. Link: [URL]."    
  }  
 }
]}
&lt;/script>
````

### Best Practices:
- Match visible text exactly.
- One per page, up to 10 items.
- **Test:** Google Rich Results.

Validate in Search Console for impressions.  

#### External Reference: [**Google FAQPage Docs**](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/faqpage).
